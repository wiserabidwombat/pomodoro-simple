# Privacy Policy for Simple: StandBy Timer

**Last updated:** September 20, 2026

Simple: StandBy Timer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What the app does

- All timer state, session history, and your accent color preference are
  stored locally on your device (and shared privately between the app and its
  Home Screen/Lock Screen widget via an iOS App Group), so your data never
  leaves your device.
- The app sends local notifications (e.g. "Focus session complete") using
  Apple's on-device notification system. These are scheduled and delivered
  entirely on your device.
- The app does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.
- The app does not create accounts, does not require sign-in, and does not
  make network requests of any kind.
- The app does not use tracking and does not share data with third parties,
  because no data is collected in the first place.

## Data collection summary

In App Store terms: **no data is collected from this app.**
